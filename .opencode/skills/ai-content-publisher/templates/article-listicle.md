## Structure for Listicle / Numbered List Articles

### Intro Section
- Hook: Why this list matters
- What the reader will gain
- Brief context

### Main Body
Each item = H2 heading as a question:

#### [Number]. [Question-Form Heading]
- Answer capsule (40-60 words)
- Detailed explanation
- Example or data point
- Practical application

#### [Number]. [Next Question-Form Heading]
- Same structure

(Continue for 5-10 items)

### How to Implement These (H2)
- Brief guide to getting started
- Prioritization advice

### FAQ Section
- 3-5 Q&A pairs

### Conclusion
- Recap of key items
- Call to action
- Internal link
