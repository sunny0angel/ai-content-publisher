## Structure for Industry Analysis Articles

### Intro Section
- Hook: The current state of the industry/trend
- Thesis: What this analysis will reveal
- Why it matters now

### Main Body (H2 sections)

#### Current Landscape (H2)
- Overview of current state
- Key players and forces (NOT product names, but categories)
- Market data/statistics

#### Key Trend 1 (H2)
- Answer capsule: What is this trend
- Evidence/data
- Impact on businesses
- Real-world example/anecdote

#### Key Trend 2 (H2)
- Same structure as Trend 1

#### Key Trend 3 (H2)
- Same structure

#### Future Outlook (H2)
- Prediction for 12-24 months
- What organizations should prepare for

### FAQ Section
- 3-5 Q&A pairs

### Conclusion
- Summary of trends
- Strategic recommendations
- Internal link
