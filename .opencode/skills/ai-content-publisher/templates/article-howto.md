## Structure for How-To / Tutorial Articles

### Intro Section
- Hook: Why this matters now (current context, 2026)
- Problem statement: What pain point this solves
- What reader will learn: 2-3 sentence summary
- Prerequisites (if any): Brief note

### Main Body (H2 sections)
Each H2 = one major step or concept:

#### Step/Concept 1
- Answer capsule (40-60 words): What is this step and why it matters
- Detailed explanation with example
- Practical tip or common pitfall

#### Step/Concept 2
- Answer capsule
- Detailed explanation
- Practical tip

(Continue for 4-6 steps total)

### Tips for Success (H2)
- 3-4 pro-tips in bullet or numbered format

### Common Mistakes to Avoid (H2)
- 2-3 mistakes with explanations

### FAQ Section
- 3-5 Q&A pairs

### Conclusion
- Summary of key takeaways
- Encouragement to start implementing
- Internal link to related article
