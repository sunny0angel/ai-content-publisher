If your team spends more than 10 hours per week in meetings and has no reliable system for capturing decisions and action items afterward, you are losing roughly one workday per week to information that vanishes the moment the call ends.

AI meeting assistants — tools that join your calls, transcribe conversations, generate summaries, and extract action items — have matured rapidly over the past 18 months. Three clear leaders have emerged: Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, and Fathom. Each takes a meaningfully different approach to pricing, accuracy, integrations, and the post-meeting workflow.

This article compares all three using independent accuracy benchmarks (openhelm.ai, PickYourAITool), verified pricing from official sites, and honest assessments of where each tool falls short. By the end, you will know exactly which tool fits your situation — and which one does not.

---

## How Large Is the AI Meeting Assistant Market in 2026?

The AI meeting assistant market is projected to reach $18 billion by 2030, growing at over 20% annually. Adoption among US businesses has crossed 40%, and these tools have shifted from experimental to essential for remote and hybrid teams.

Three factors drive adoption:

1. **Meeting volume is not decreasing.** The average knowledge worker attends 12-16 meetings per week, and 57% report that meeting follow-up is inconsistent or nonexistent. This pattern mirrors the broader [AI adoption trends across business functions](/blog/ai-adoption-report-2026/), where tools that address high-frequency pain points see the fastest adoption rates.
2. **Transcription quality crossed the usability threshold.** In 2022, AI transcription was good enough for rough notes. In 2026, it is good enough for legal-grade documentation — Fathom and Otter both exceed 90% accuracy on clean audio.
3. **LLMs changed what is possible post-meeting.** Modern tools extract action items, detect sentiment, generate follow-up emails, and push structured data into CRMs and project management tools automatically.

The three leaders — Otter, Fireflies, and Fathom — collectively serve over 5 million users. But they serve different use cases, and picking the wrong one wastes both money and team adoption energy.

## How Accurate Are AI Meeting Transcription Tools?

Accuracy is the foundational metric. If transcription is wrong, everything built on top — summaries, action items, search — is wrong too.

![Independent benchmark results across 500 meetings: Fathom achieves 95% transcription accuracy on clean audio, Fireflies 92%, Otter 91%. Data from openhelm.ai, hand-checked against 50 randomly sampled transcripts.](/images/charts/ai-meeting-2026-chart-1.png "Transcription accuracy on clean audio: Fathom 95%, Fireflies 92%, Otter 91%. Source: openhelm.ai (500 meetings, hand-checked 50 calls).")

The most rigorous independent test comes from openhelm.ai, which ran eight tools across 500 meetings over three months and hand-checked transcripts against 50 randomly sampled calls. On clean audio — good microphones, minimal background noise, standard English accents — the results show a clear hierarchy:

| Tool | Accuracy (Clean Audio) | Accuracy (Noisy or Accented) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fathom | 95% | ~70% |
| Fireflies | 92% | ~65% |
| Otter | 91% | ~60% |

**The chart above shows that Fathom holds a measurable lead — roughly 3 points over Fireflies and 4 points over Otter.** A second independent test from PickYourAITool, running all three tools on identical sales calls and internal meetings, found similar results: Fathom 95.2%, Otter 93.8%, Fireflies 91.4%.

The critical caveat: under difficult conditions — heavy accents, crosstalk, background noise — all three drop to 60-70%. None is reliable for critical transcription in noisy environments.

**Accuracy winner: Fathom.** But the gaps are smaller than marketing claims suggest. For a typical business meeting with decent audio, all three produce transcripts good enough for summary generation.

## How Do Pricing Models Compare Across Tools?

Pricing reveals the three tools' fundamentally different strategies. Headline numbers hide important details.

![Monthly cost per user on Pro plans with annual billing: Otter $8.33, Fireflies $10.00, Fathom $16.00. Fathom's higher per-user cost reflects unlimited recording included in all plans.](/images/charts/ai-meeting-2026-chart-2.png "Pro plan monthly cost per user (annual billing): Otter $8.33, Fireflies $10, Fathom $16.")

### Otter.ai Pricing (2026)

| Plan | Monthly (Annual) | Key Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 300 min/mo, 30 min/meeting, 3 lifetime file imports |
| Pro | $8.33/user/mo | 1,200 min/mo, 90 min/meeting, 10 file imports/mo |
| Business | $19.99/user/mo | Unlimited meetings, 4 hr/meeting, admin analytics |
| Enterprise | Custom | SSO, HIPAA (add-on), API |

Otter is the cheapest paid entry at $8.33/user/month. But the free tier is severely constrained — 300 minutes per month with a 30-minute per-meeting cap makes it impractical beyond a few short meetings per week. The Pro tier's 90-minute per-meeting cap excludes longer workshops and training sessions.

### Fireflies.ai Pricing (2026)

| Plan | Monthly (Annual) | Key Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Unlimited transcription, 800 min storage/team |
| Pro | $10/user/mo | 8,000 min storage/seat, 20 AI credits (one-time) |
| Business | $19/user/mo | Unlimited storage, 30 AI credits |
| Enterprise | $39/user/mo | Unlimited, 50 AI credits, SSO |

Fireflies introduces a hidden cost: **AI credits**. The Pro plan includes 20 one-time AI credits — not per month, one-time ever. Advanced features consume credits per use. When exhausted, you buy more at $5 per 50 credits. For a sales team running 50 calls per month with CRM autofill, the effective cost becomes $10/user (base) plus $5-20/user (credits). The free tier is more generous than Otter (unlimited transcription, 800 minutes storage) but fills quickly for active teams.

### Fathom Pricing (2026)

| Plan | Monthly (Annual) | Key Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Unlimited recording and transcription, 5 AI summaries/mo |
| Premium | $16/user/mo | Unlimited advanced summaries, action items, Ask Fathom |
| Team | $15/user/mo | Team search, SSO (2-user minimum) |
| Business | $25/user/mo | CRM sync, deal view, coaching scorecards |

Fathom's free plan is the most generous in the category: **truly unlimited** recording and transcription — no caps, no per-meeting limits, no storage ceilings. The trade-off: AI summaries are limited to 5 per month. For light users who only need recordings and basic note-taking, the free plan is genuinely usable indefinitely.

Pricing quirk: Team ($15/user/mo annual) is cheaper than Premium ($16/user/mo annual) and includes everything Premium has plus team features. If you have even two users, Team is the better buy.

The pricing picture shifts for teams:

![Total monthly cost for a 5-person team with annual billing: Fathom Team $75, Fireflies Business $95, Otter Business $99.95. Fathom's Team plan at $75/month for 5 users is the cheapest team option. Fireflies Business at $95/month is competitive but requires AI credits for advanced features. Otter Business at $99.95/month is the most expensive and still lacks CRM sync (Enterprise only).

**Cheapest paid entry: Otter Pro** at $8.33/user/month.
**Best free tier: Fathom Free** — unlimited recording with no credit card required.
**Best team value: Fathom Team** at $15/user/month ($75 total for 5).

## How Do Features Compare Across Meeting Assistants?

### AI Summaries and Templates

The post-meeting summary is where practical value differs most.

![AI summary templates available by platform: Fathom leads with 17 structured templates (BANT, MEDDICC, SANDLER, retro, Q&A, and more), Fireflies offers 8 via AI Skills (credit-gated), Otter provides 2 (Outline and full transcript).](/images/charts/ai-meeting-2026-chart-5.png "Summary templates available: Fathom 17, Fireflies 8 (AI Skills), Otter 2.")

| Dimension | Otter | Fireflies | Fathom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Summary formats | Outline + full transcript | Smart Notes + AI Skills templates | 17 templates (BANT, MEDDICC, SANDLER, 1:1, retro) |
| Action item extraction | Adequate | Good (86/88/79 on tests) | Excellent (89/92/84 on tests) |
| Cross-meeting search | AI Chat + Channels (excellent) | AskFred + 200 AI Skills | Ask Fathom (cited, timestamp-linked) |
| Summary speed | 2-5 minutes post-call | 2-5 minutes post-call | ~30 seconds post-call |

Fathom's 17 summary templates produce the most structured output out of the box. Sales teams get frameworks like SANDLER, SPICED, MEDDPICC, and BANT. Customer success teams get Q&A and retrospective templates. Each generates consistent, professional notes without editing.

Fireflies' AI Skills offers more templates (200+) but gates them behind AI credits. On Free and Pro tiers, the most useful templates require credit spend.

Otter's two formats (Outline and full transcript) are the least structured. The Outline captures decisions and action items but misses nuance.

**Action item extraction** — arguably the most practically important feature — shows a clear gap. In openhelm's test, Fathom led on recall (89%), precision (92%), and attribution accuracy (84%). Fireflies came second (86/88/79). Otter was weakest on attribution.

**Summary speed** matters more than most reviews acknowledge. Fathom delivers a structured summary in about 30 seconds — fast enough to send a follow-up email while the conversation is fresh. Otter and Fireflies both take 2-5 minutes. In practice, the 30-second turnaround changes behavior: users actually send follow-ups immediately instead of procrastinating.

**Winner: Fathom** for summary quality, speed, and template depth.

Otter gates CRM sync behind Enterprise, making it the least integration-friendly option for teams that need notes flowing automatically into their CRM.

**Winner: Fireflies** for integration depth.

## Security, Privacy, and Compliance: How Do the Tools Compare?

For organizations handling sensitive conversations — legal, healthcare, financial services, or internal strategy meetings — security considerations often outweigh feature comparisons. The three tools take meaningfully different approaches to data handling, compliance certifications, and meeting bot visibility.

### Data Storage and Retention

Otter retains recordings and transcripts on its servers indefinitely on paid plans, with the option to delete individual meetings. Enterprise plans offer custom retention policies. Fireflies stores data in encrypted cloud storage with adjustable retention periods — Pro users can set auto-deletion windows from 30 days to indefinite. Fathom offers the most granular controls: users can set organization-wide retention policies, automatically delete recordings after a specified period, and configure per-meeting privacy settings.

### Compliance Certifications

| Certification | Otter | Fireflies | Fathom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SOC 2 Type II | Enterprise only | Yes | Yes |
| HIPAA | Add-on (Enterprise) | Enterprise only | Enterprise only |
| GDPR | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Data encryption at rest | Yes | Yes | AES-256 |
| Single sign-on (SSO) | Enterprise | Enterprise | Team+ plans |

Fireflies and Fathom both hold SOC 2 Type II certification, meaning an independent auditor has verified their security controls. Otter offers SOC 2 only to Enterprise customers, which is a meaningful gap for mid-market companies.

### Meeting Bot Visibility

A growing concern among organizations is whether an AI assistant joining meetings is visible to all participants. Fathom gives the most granular control: users can choose per-meeting between bot-free mode (the tool records audio from your device without joining as a participant), full bot presence (visible to all), or audio-only capture. Otter's desktop app similarly supports bot-free recording. Fireflies is primarily bot-first, which means participants see the Fireflies bot join the call. For internal all-hands meetings or confidential client calls, the level of bot visibility may determine which tool is appropriate.

### Who Owns the Transcripts?

This is often overlooked but critical: on all three platforms, the person who records the meeting owns the transcript and can share it. Fathom and Fireflies both allow workspace admins to set default sharing rules — for example, automatically sharing all transcripts with meeting participants. Otter requires manual sharing. Organizations with strict data governance requirements should evaluate whether their chosen tool allows automated compliance with internal data classification policies.

**Winner for security: Fathom** for the best combination of SOC 2 certification, SSO on Team plans, granular retention controls, and flexible bot presence settings.

## Which Tool Wins for Each Use Case?

### Solo Professional or Freelancer

Unlimited recording, clean summaries, low or no cost.

**Fathom Free.** The unlimited recording and 5 free AI summaries per month cover most solo needs. If you need more summaries, Premium ($16/mo) delivers template-based output worth the upgrade. Otter's 300-minute cap and Fireflies' AI credit system both create unnecessary friction for solo users.

### Sales Team (5-20 Reps)

CRM integration, call coaching, deal intelligence, team-wide search.

**Fireflies Business** ($19/user/mo) if your CRM is Salesforce or HubSpot and you want automated call logging. The AI credits system is annoying but manageable at team scale. **Fathom Business** ($25/user/mo) if you prioritize summary quality over integration breadth — the coaching scorecards and deal view are genuinely useful for sales managers.

### Engineering or Product Team

Fast summaries, action item tracking, Slack integration, no sales overhead features.

**Fathom Team** ($15/user/mo). The 30-second summary speed and action item extraction quality are the best fit for standups, sprint reviews, and retros. The 2-user minimum is rarely an issue for teams.

### Accessibility or Lecture Use

Real-time captions during meetings, pre-recorded audio import, live collaborative notes.

**Otter** is the only tool here that offers real-time live transcription with collaborative in-meeting annotation. It also handles pre-recorded audio file imports — a capability Fireflies supports partially and Fathom does not support at all. If you record interviews on a voice recorder or need post-hoc transcription of offline recordings, Otter is the tool.

### Large Enterprise (100+ Employees)

SSO, compliance (HIPAA, SOC 2), admin controls, data retention policies.

**Fathom Enterprise or Fireflies Enterprise.** Both offer SSO, SOC 2 Type II, and admin analytics. Otter Enterprise adds HIPAA as an add-on with custom pricing. Fathom's Business plan ($25/user/mo) includes SSO without requiring an Enterprise contract, a meaningful advantage for mid-size companies.

## The Honest Assessment: Which Tool Should You Actually Choose?

**Choose Fathom if:** You are a solo professional, small team, or mid-size company that wants the best summary quality, fastest post-meeting turnaround, and the most generous free plan. Fathom is the best overall pick for most people in 2026.

**Choose Fireflies if:** You run a sales or customer success team that lives in Salesforce or HubSpot, or your meetings happen across multiple languages. The AI credit system is frustrating, but the integration depth and language coverage are unmatched.

**Choose Otter if:** You need real-time live captions during meetings (accessibility, legal, education), you record interviews or lectures offline and need transcription, or you want the most searchable archive of past meetings for under $10/user/month.

**Do not choose any of them if:** You need reliable transcription in noisy environments with heavy accents or frequent crosstalk. None exceeds 70% accuracy under those conditions. A human notetaker or dedicated transcription service (e.g., Rev) is still the right tool for that use case.

A practical note on tool evaluation: the best approach is to run a 14-day trial of your top two candidates simultaneously on real meetings. Start with Fathom Free (no credit card required) alongside either Otter Free or Fireflies Free. After two weeks of parallel use, compare which tool's summaries you actually read, which action items you find actionable, and whether the integration with your existing Slack or CRM workflow feels natural rather than forced. Most teams find the decision becomes obvious within 10 meetings rather than 100.
