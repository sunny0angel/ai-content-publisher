## How it works

The tool draws your image onto a canvas, resizes it to your maximum width, and re-encodes it as JPEG at your chosen quality. The output size shown is computed from the data length, so the before/after savings are honest. PNG transparency is flattened to JPEG — for lossless output use the [image format converter](/tools/image-converter/).

## Tips for smaller files

- **Quality 70–80** is visually near-lossless for photos and dramatically smaller
- **Max width 1200–1600** covers most web use; social uploads rarely need more
- **Screenshots compress best as PNG→JPG** only when they are photos; keep text-heavy screenshots in PNG
