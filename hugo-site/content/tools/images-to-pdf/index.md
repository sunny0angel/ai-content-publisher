## How it works

Each image is embedded at full resolution and scaled to fit the chosen page size with its aspect ratio intact. JPG and PNG are supported; transparency is preserved for PNG. For a document-first workflow, merge it with an existing PDF using [merge PDF](/tools/pdf-merge/).

## Good to know

- Select images in the order you want the pages
- A4 portrait suits documents; A4 landscape suits slides and wide photos
- Reduce 20 MB phone photos first with the [image compressor](/tools/image-compressor/)
